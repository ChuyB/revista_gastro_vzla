About Makenna

A teacher who values real communication and cultural connection.

This website is designed to feel personal, welcoming, and easy to navigate, with a friendly tone rather than a corporate one. It highlights Makenna's teaching background, experience in Japan, and focus on natural English communication. fileciteturn1file0L1-L31

I believe students learn best when lessons feel comfortable, practical, and encouraging. My teaching style focuses on helping learners express themselves more naturally while building confidence through conversation, listening practice, pronunciation support, and useful everyday expressions.

I have experience working with international learners and a strong interest in language learning, daily communication, and cultural exchange. Having lived in Osaka and Hyogo, I understand the value of creating lessons that feel relevant, human, and enjoyable.
